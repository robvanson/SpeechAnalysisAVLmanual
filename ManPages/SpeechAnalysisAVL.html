Speech Analysis AVL

Praat script that combines a number of scripts to analyse voicing, voice quality, and vowel spaces.

There are 4 scripts available:

• AVQI - Acoustic Voice Quality Index
Calculates voice quality on a scale of 0-10 based on a sustained vowel (3s) and continuous speech (4s) Only available if separate AVQI script is available
• Vowel Triangle
Plots a vowel triangle of the vowels in a speech recording
• Pitch and Intensity ranges
A simplified phonetogram (Intensity x F0)
• Vowel Bender
Converts typical speech into hemiglossectomy speech with a restricted vowel space
